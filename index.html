About Dr. Ritu Agrawal

Renowned Obstetrician & Gynaecologist

MBBS • MD • DGO | Banaras Hindu University

Dr. Ritu Agrawal is a renowned Obstetrician and Gynaecologist with over 28 years of clinical experience, dedicated to providing comprehensive, compassionate, and personalised care to women at every stage of life.

She specialises in high-risk pregnancies, complex deliveries, infertility care, laparoscopic hysterectomy, open & vaginal surgeries, always focusing on patient safety and excellent clinical outcomes.
