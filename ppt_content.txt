Laparoscopy & Keyhole Surgery
Laparoscopy, also known as keyhole surgery, is an important part of modern gynaecological care. It offers significant advantages in the management of infertility and a wide range of gynaecological conditions, including reduced hospital stay, less postoperative discomfort, and faster recovery.
For the surgeon, laparoscopic procedures provide enhanced magnification and precision, along with reduced blood loss in many procedures. Fertility-enhancing procedures, ovarian surgeries, and hysterectomies can be performed using advanced laparoscopic techniques when clinically appropriate.
With access to a well-equipped operating theatre and a skilled, experienced surgical team, Dr. Ritu Agrawal focuses on delivering safe, precise, and effective minimally invasive gynaecological care with the aim of achieving excellent clinical outcomes.

Ultrasound in Obstetrics & Gynaecology
Ultrasound is an essential diagnostic tool in modern Obstetrics and Gynaecology, offering valuable insights for accurate assessment and monitoring. It is routinely used for antenatal monitoring, pregnancy assessment, and infertility evaluation.
With access to advanced ultrasound technology and experienced clinical expertise, patients can benefit from accurate evaluation of a wide range of obstetric and gynaecological conditions. Ultrasound also supports routine and interventional procedures, helping enable precise diagnosis and appropriate treatment planning.


--- Slide 13 ---
Cancer Screening
Early detection of cancer can significantly improve treatment outcomes, particularly when precancerous changes or cancer are identified at an early stage. Regular screening plays an important role in the prevention and early detection of common gynaecological and breast cancers.
Cancer screening and evaluation may include Pap smears, colposcopy, mammography, and other appropriate investigations, based on the patient’s age, symptoms, risk factors, and clinical history. Screening and treatment are planned in accordance with established international guidelines and evidence-based medical practices.





--- Slide 14 ---
Infertility Treatment
Couples experiencing infertility receive comprehensive evaluation, appropriate investigations, and personalised treatment based on their individual needs. The approach focuses on identifying the underlying cause and offering the most suitable fertility-enhancing options.

Laparoscopic and hysteroscopic procedures for fertility enhancement are routinely performed when clinically indicated. Our operating theatres are equipped with advanced surgical technology, including Harmonic Scalpel, high-definition cameras, and specialised laparoscopic instruments, supporting precise and minimally invasive procedures.

Patients have access to a multidisciplinary team of fertility and assisted reproductive medicine specialists, who provide evidence-based care across a range of treatment options, including IUI and IVF, as well as advanced hysteroscopic and laparoscopic surgeries where required.

The fertility care team includes experienced embryologists, counsellors, dieticians, and physiotherapists, providing coordinated support throughout the treatment journey.
